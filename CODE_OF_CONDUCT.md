# Code of Conduct

NumeralSystems.Net is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and technically
constructive community.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- treat people with empathy and respect;
- discuss ideas and code without attacking the person;
- give actionable feedback and accept it professionally;
- acknowledge mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from them;
- respect privacy, boundaries, and differing levels of experience;
- prioritize the health of the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation;
- discriminatory or sexualized language, imagery, or attention;
- insults, trolling, deliberate disruption, or personal and political attacks;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is officially
representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[mi@polecola.it](mailto:mi@polecola.it).
Include relevant links or records and explain any immediate safety concern.

Project maintainers will review reports as promptly and impartially as
practical, protect the reporter's privacy, and avoid conflicts of interest.
Depending on impact and history, responses may include clarification, a private
warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban.

Maintainers may remove or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that do not follow this policy. Deliberately false or bad-faith
reports may themselves violate this policy.
